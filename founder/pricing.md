# Pricing — Eat Out Better (2026-10-12)

## 1. The price: test $24.99/yr, keep $29.99 as the ceiling, drop $39.99 for now

- **Buyers (simulated, n=20):** acceptable range **$5.08 to $34.97**, indifference point **$20.01**. Median answers: bargain $15, getting expensive $30, too expensive $45. The planned $39.99 is **above** the range ([pricing-curve.md](pricing-curve.md)).
- **Competitors:** annual plans run from $19.99 (Dishup, MenuFit) to $39.99 (Fig), with a median around $29.99 ([competitors.md](competitors.md)). At $39.99, Eat Out Better would be the most expensive menu scanner while being the newest, with zero ratings.
- **Margin (CFO tool):** $19.99 gives a 48% margin at plan and year 1 profit of $9,283. $24.99 gives 58% and $15,658. $29.99 gives 65% and $22,033. $39.99 gives 74% and $34,783. Variable cost is about $9 per annual subscription, so even $19.99 clears it comfortably.
- **Recommendation:** launch at **$24.99/yr as the founding price** (inside the range, above the $20 indifference point, below the $30 "expensive" median), with **$34.99/yr** as the list price after 90 days, at the top of the acceptable range. Monthly at $4.99 is the decoy.

The honest caveat: **price is not why the panel said no.** 0 of 20 bought, and only 2 of the 20 said price was the reason. They passed on habit (7), trust (5), need (4) and convenience (2). A lower price won't fix that. It only removes a second objection.

## 2. The ladder

| Rung | Price | Why step up |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | First scan fully unlocked, then the top 3 picks on every menu, with reasons |
| Plus annual | $24.99 founding → $34.99 | Every dish, "What to ask for", history, scanning at home from a menu link or PDF |
| Lifetime | $59.99 | For the 3 of 20 who hate auto-renewal (Darius, Malik, Callum). It answers the subscription objection directly |
| Week pass | $3.99, no renewal | Travel diners and holidays. Defer to Phase 3 as in growth-plan.md |

Lifetime gets promoted from "anchor" to a real product, because the subscription-averse group named "no auto-renew" as what would flip them.

## 3. The opening offer

Founding $24.99/yr for waitlist members and the first 90 days after launch (until **8 Mar 2027**). It's held for as long as the subscription continues, with no fake "was" price. The trial reminder goes out 2 days before the charge.

## 4. What to test with real buyers

Run the Google Search pre-order test (growth-plan.md, Nov) **at two prices, $24.99 and $34.99**, split 50/50 on two landing pages with refundable pre-orders. Pass: cost per pre-order under $20 at either price. If $34.99 converts within 70% of $24.99, launch at $34.99.

## 5. The panel's price objections (verbatim, for marketing)

1. "I wouldn't pay $39.99 a year for something I can mostly get by asking ChatGPT with a photo of the menu." (P001)
2. "Another subscription for something I use twice a month." (P018)
3. "I also cancel anything that auto-renews after a trial." (P007)

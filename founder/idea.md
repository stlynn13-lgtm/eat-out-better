# Eat Out Better — the idea (for the founder skills)

iPhone app. Photograph a restaurant menu; get every dish rated green / yellow / red for high cholesterol, with the reason (ingredients, cooking method). v1 is cholesterol only. Not yet on the App Store: TestFlight build 1.5.0, waitlist live at eatoutbetter.com, public launch targeted Tue 8 Dec 2026 if the repeat-use test passes (gate Fri 13 Nov). Proposed pricing (unvalidated): free tier, Plus $39.99/yr, founding $29.99/yr. Scan cost ~$0.04. Company: Dine Right LLC, Colorado.

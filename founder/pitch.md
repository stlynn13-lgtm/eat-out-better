Eat Out Better, a new iPhone app for people with high cholesterol.
At a restaurant, take a photo of the menu. In about 20 seconds every dish is marked green, yellow or red for cholesterol, with the reason (for example: "fried, cream sauce").
Your first scan is fully free. After that, the top 3 picks on any menu stay free; seeing every dish and saved history is Plus, $39.99 a year (about $3.33 a month), with a 7-day free trial.
Launch offer for early users: $29.99 a year, kept as long as you stay subscribed.
It does not replace medical advice and does not count exact grams.
